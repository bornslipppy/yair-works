Impact Model:
20 years across B2B, fintech, operations, and consumer at Principal level. I pair strategy and design judgment with hands-on execution — designing in code and building self-improving agentic systems that make each iteration better than the last.

I lead through the work—shaping decisions, prototyping ideas, designing behaviour, and defining the systems that help teams understand what to build and why. I bring judgment to ambiguous, fast-changing environments, cutting through competing priorities and opinions to focus on what matters most.

I create momentum by making decisions, surfacing trade-offs, and keeping work from stalling or drifting, without sacrificing quality. The result is thoughtful, resilient product work that holds up across edge cases, scales under real constraints, and gives teams the clarity and confidence to deliver.
